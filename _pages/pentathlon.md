Enter your results in each event to calculate your point score in the Modern Pentathlon. There is a 'par standard' in each event which gives 1,000 points (or 2,000 for the Laser Run). To achieve a Full Blue, you must score 5,200 points (to achieve a Half Blue, you must achieve 4,600 points). The description of the point-scoring system is explained beneath each event.
